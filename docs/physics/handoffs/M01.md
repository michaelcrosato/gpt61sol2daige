Push right and off-center controls change solved position/rotation; the sweep predicts contact and launches a real body. Pause/run/step, agent commands, JSON snapshots, saved trails and replay share the same state.

Reset, close, simulation replacement, failed replay and disposal release the old world/event queue. Browser unload cancels the queued animation before disposal. Initialization errors display reload guidance; canceling a waiter cannot cancel another or allocate an abandoned world. Adventure actors remain on their existing solver. The playground rejects online and connecting sessions; rooms reject hosting/joining while it is open.
