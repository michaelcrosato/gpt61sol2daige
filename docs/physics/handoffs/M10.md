# M10 handoff

## Delivered behavior

Towns and the eight authored areas now show off the reactive world, and every effect stays optional.

- **Town Sanctuary.** Each town has a market:
  - three stalls with awnings on sprung hinges, and four lamps on bracket arms that swing when slashed or bumped and settle again;
  - a bunting line fluttering in a breeze;
  - baskets, crates, a barrel and pots to shove around.

  The town runs on the Sanctuary values (shoves and swings, never breaks), and its market region keeps goods solid. Permanent repel rings keep the hearth, the gate and each townsperson's home clear, even when the town is switched Reactive and every good is piled on them. Rest, shop, respec and the outward gate work by real input.
- **One physical extension per area**, from the `SHOWCASE` recipes, on three instances of each mechanic:

  | Area | Extension | Measured (receipt) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 1 Brambleburst | Pod burst: shove, 8 owned thorn splinters, hedges break, cache opens | shove 28.8 (wild 47.9, calm 8.9 base hit only); 3/3 hedges, 0 in calm |
  | 2 Slipstream | Tailwind lanes carry travelers, monsters and props; crossing adds an owned gust; vanes driven | barrels 92.7 and 95.2 units in 2 s, calm 0 |
  | 3 Stormglass | Permanent pools soak waders; a hero strike on a wet monster arcs through the wet pack | 10 arcs, neighbour −56 HP |
  | 4 Echo Wells | The well repeats the ability's own force, cause `echo:<ability>`, paid once | stones 47.3 → 129.6 units/s, 0 extra gold |
  | 5 Cinderwake | Crossing a vent ignites its fuse into a weakened stockade that burns open | 4/4 boards, calm 0 |
  | 6 Bloodbloom | The sacrifice grows elastic vines to up to six monsters, dragging and holding them | 6 vines, pulled in 36–72 units |
  | 7 Gravity Knots | A struck knot drifts along the blow, carrying monsters, material and loot as one cluster | material 132, pack 117, spread 9 |
  | 8 Riftstep | Freight on the arch's pad travels with the traveler and bursts out on arrival unharmed | 3/3 carried, 3/3 survive, 91–117 units outward |

  Area 9 combines the set pieces of all three of its mechanics.
- **Calm and wild regions inside each area.** The signature mechanic's second instance is wild (Wild values) and the third calm (Quiet values, world reactions on). Both are drawn as rings on the ground.
- **All eight wardens** telegraph a signature for 64 ticks with a locked target (ring, lane, line or marker): thornburst, gale charge, discharge, echo slam, breath, lash, collapse and blink. Each can be exposed (1.5× damage, stagger, telegraph broken) by its own area's rule; see the [ARCHITECTURE table](../../ARCHITECTURE.md#m10-reactive-towns-and-authored-areas).
- **Guidance.**
  - The atlas notes each area's extension and warden, and draws the calm and wild rings.
  - The mechanic guide explains the physical extension, the warden and the regions.
  - Prompts cover the bloom and rift freight, and the HUD shows the signature with its dodge hint or EXPOSED.
  - New sounds for telegraphs, vines, freight and exposure.
  - The QA bot steers around solid scenery.
