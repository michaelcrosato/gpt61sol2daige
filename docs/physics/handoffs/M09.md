# M09 handoff

## Delivered behavior

All six monster rigs (stalker, crawler, brute, wraith, totem, warden) are now physical rigs with their own hit, stagger, knockdown and death responses.

- **Rigs.** Each rig is a tree of parts: socket, mass share, hinge limits, material and its own pixel art. Collider boxes are measured from that art. Locomotion and attacks stay authored (legs, arms, tails, antennae and head channels, windup lift and charge thrust).
- **Hits.** A blow leans the figure away from it, and the parts follow with weight and lag within their limits. Poise builds per blow, and past a rig's thresholds the monster staggers (its windup or charge is cut short) or is knocked down: no steering or attacks until it rises.
  - Stalkers fall over and crawlers flip onto their backs.
  - Wraiths sway furthest and scatter, then gather again.
  - Brutes and wardens barely tip.
  - Rooted totems are barely pushed and only teeter; they never fall.
  - Bosses only stagger.
- **Shedding.** Heavy blows knock bark plates (brute, wood), armor plates and an emblem (warden, metal) and crown spikes (totem, wood) off as loose material props. The wraith's glass lantern core comes loose at death.
- **Death.** The living actor leaves the solver. In the same tick its drawn pose becomes a jointed ragdoll lying where the art falls, carrying the body's velocity and the killing blow:
  - stalker, brute and warden topple sideways about the feet;
  - the crawler flips;
  - the wraith's shroud slumps and spreads;
  - the totem's trunk is felled while its roots stay planted.

  It lands with a material thud. Whorl, fields, blasts, grabs and passing bodies move it. Burning, wet, oiled or charged monsters pass that to their remains. Remains last 45 s.
- **Controls.**
  - `ragdolls` off freezes a ragdoll where it lies (a non-reactive corpse); where it is already off at death, the authored death pose plays instead.
  - `foliage` turns the trees' and brush's saved bend on and off.
  - `reactionStrength` scales all of the above.
  - Each control works per land, area and region, with presets and panel controls.
  - Camera shake and hit/blast flashes are local Settings preferences.
- **Wayfarer and town.** The wayfarer leans with a blow and its lantern swings on the hand; the cloak hem trails motion; input and movement are untouched. Townsfolk are physical: a traveler shoves them, they lean back with a startled mark and walk back to their post, and their services follow them.
- **Feedback.** New rig events: stagger, topple, shed, fall, npc bump. Thud and flutter voices, material voices for shed armor, screen shake and flashes, glowing cores and slits, and ground shadows that stretch with a fallen body.

## Contracts and source

- **`src/game/rigs.ts`** (new, engine-safe):
  - `RIG_BLUEPRINTS` (parts, death style, locomotion, springs, poise thresholds, knockback, hover, mass, fall ticks) and `rigArt`;
  - `rigGeometry` (art-measured boxes), `rigPose` and `enemyPose` (the one pose function for renderer and death transfer);
  - `fallAngle` and `fallPivot`, `stepReaction` and `hitReaction` (`POISE_PER_BLOW = 10`), `stepRecoil` and `recoilHit`, and `rigExport`.
- **`Enemy.reaction`** and **`Hero.recoil`** are new authoritative state. Validation accepts them absent, and `Adventure.restore` fills fresh defaults. `Adventure.rigHit` applies reactions in `hit()`, a knocked-down monster skips AI, and `spawnMonster` and `strikeEnemy` are QA/agent entry points.
- **`src/physics/rigs.ts`** (new):
  - `remainsRecipes` builds bodies, limited hinges (`remains-<enemy>:<part>`) and loose pieces from a pose;
  - `RigPhysics` holds remains records, landing events, foliage bend and pending rig events;
  - `REMAINS_TICKS = 2700`.
- **`AdventurePhysics`**:
  - `retire` (death transfer at the first boundary after a kill, from `begin` or `solve`), `shed`, `townsfolk` (town-only `npc-<id>` actors), `npcPoint` and `updateRigs` (bumps, landing, foliage);
  - expiry removes remains with `PhysicsWorld.removeAssembly`;
  - land archives exclude remains.
- **`PhysicsWorld`**:
  - remains collision bit (512) and dominance −1;
  - `frozenByPolicy` (ragdoll members freeze on `!ragdolls`);
  - townsfolk always contact players;
  - `removeAssembly` (remains only) and `motorOf`.
- **Blueprints**: family `remains` with a validated `rig` tag (`RemainsTag`); `variantName` names the creature. Assembly kind `remains`.
- **Policies**: `ragdolls` and `foliage` (gated by world reactions) and `reactionStrength` (0–10), with presets (Quiet off/off, Sanctuary 0.35, Wild 2) and panel controls.
- **Versions** (D68): physical world **9** (older raw bytes rebuild semantically), adventure envelope **7** (`rigs`), room protocol **10**. A real M08 checkpoint (`tests/fixtures/m08-raw.json.gz`, captured from `main` `a53af8d`) migrates.
- **Agent**: `actors` actions `rigs`, `monster` (`rig`, `x`, `y`, `hp`, `passive`, `boss`, `clear`) and `hit`, all in `describe`. `observe().render.feedback` reports the last frame's shake and flash.
- **Presentation**:
  - `src/render/rigs.ts` (part-image cache; living, dying and remains drawing);
  - `combat.ts` (rigs and townsfolk) and `renderer.ts` (remains as one y-sorted item, wayfarer lean, lantern and cloak, shake and flash);
  - `props.ts` (foliage bend);
  - `synth.ts` (`thud`, `flutter`), Settings (`cameraShake`, `hitFlash`).
  - `main.ts` key taps: a key pressed and released between two frames is latched until a tick consumes it (or a guest sends it), so it acts once. `Coop.update` reports whether a guest sent its input.
- **Decisions**: D61–D68 in [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md); policy semantics in [POLICIES.md](../POLICIES.md#m09-delivered-controls); contract in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md#m09-physical-rigs-and-expressive-reactions).
