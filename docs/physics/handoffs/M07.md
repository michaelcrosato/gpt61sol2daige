# M07 handoff

## Delivered behavior

Every adventure area now has six jointed mechanisms in its north meadow and along its west edge. They are built from connected physical parts and stay out of required travel:
- **Swinging gate** on a sprung hinge, closing a fence pen with a cache chest inside. Push or pull it past 83° and it latches open; push it back and it swings shut.
- **Iron ball on a four-link chain**. Whorl it into monsters, yank or throw it, or cut it free; it flies on with its own motion.
- **Vine** of six rope-linked segments holding a seed pod. The tether limits how far the pod moves until it is cut or yanked hard enough to snap.
- **Sprung launcher**. Grab the sled, pull it back against its spring and let go: it fires, throwing whatever lies in front of it (a stone by default) as the cocker's.
- **Raised wind vane** turning on a driven pivot. Whorl and blows spin it; travelers and shots pass under it.
- **Four-plank causeway** across a new wadeable creek. Travelers on an anchored plank do not wade, about twice as fast as wading. Cut a lashing and the causeway swings away.

Attacks cut the nearest joint of the part they hit. Strain from yanks, throws, holds and heavy pushes snaps joints past their break load; free swinging never does. The `mechanisms`, `jointBreakage` and `jointStrength` region policies freeze, protect or weaken whole connected parts, resolved at each part's root.

## Contracts and source

- **Supported joint types** (`src/physics/types.ts`, `runtime.ts`), all Rapier impulse joints:
  - `hinge`: revolute; optional angle limits and motor.
  - `fixed`: rigid attachment at an authored relative angle.
  - `rope`: tether, distance at most `length`.
  - `spring`: damped, toward its rest `length`.
  - `slider`: prismatic along `axis`; optional limits and motor.

  Every `JointRecipe {id "<assembly>:<name>", kind, assembly, a, b, anchorA, anchorB, frame?, length?, stiffness?, damping?, limits?, axis?, motor?, breakLoad, toughness}` belongs to an `AssemblyRecipe {id, kind: gate|chain|vine|launcher|vane|bridge|lab, areaId, root, members, event: none|launch|deck|latch}`. Members name their assembly in `BodyRecipe.assembly`. A `JointEntry` adds `handle` (−1 once broken), `broken`, cut `damage`, `load`, `peak`, current `motor`, and `brokenAt`/`cause`.
- **Rapier usage** (D47): joints are created with no wake-up and no setter. Limits and motors are Fern pre-solve velocity rules (`driveJoints`):
  - a position motor is a damped spring (stiffness 1/s², damping 1/s);
  - a velocity motor approaches its speed at rate `damping` (1/s);
  - limits stop motion exactly at them;
  - changing a part's spin keeps its linear velocity consistent with its pin.

  Authored gaps keep linked parts from touching; vanes are raised and meet nothing. `projectJoints` restores pins and rope lengths after each solve (D48).
- **Stress approximation** (D46): Rapier 0.21 exposes no joint impulses, so load is estimated before each solve.
  - Load is Σ mass × (speed away from the joint's anchor, relative to the anchor) over the joint's connected part.
  - Ropes count only when taut, springs only past 2.5× rest, sliders only across their axis.
  - A joint snaps when load > `breakLoad × jointStrength` and `jointBreakage` is on.
  - Cuts: a damaging attack on a member cuts the intact joint of that member nearest the attack's origin, by the member material's damage after resistance. It severs at `toughness × jointStrength`.
  - Measured examples: a held gate dragged away loads its hinge to about 1,060 (break load 1,400); a 700 units/s yank on the chained ball reaches 3,054 at the anchor (2,600); a vine pull at 300 units/s reaches 169 (260).
- **Assembly recipes** (`src/physics/mechanisms.ts`, `areaMechanisms`): each recipe lists its joints with break load and cut toughness (`MECHANISMS` holds the summaries, `mechanismExport()` the registry for `actors/mechanisms`).
  - gate: hinge ±1.9 rad, return spring 6/s² and 4/s, 1,400 / 45.
  - chain: anchor 2,600 / 60; links and ball 3,200 / 90.
  - vine: ropes 260 / 8.
  - launcher: slider −13..+8 and a spring (stiffness 2,000, damping 4), spring cut 40.
  - vane: hinge driven at 1.4 rad/s, unbreakable.
  - bridge: lashings 900 / 25, deck joints 1,300 / 40.
- **Companions**: the latch post, three pen fence walls and an 18-gold chest (all destructible), and the launcher's stone payload. Areas gain a creek: shallow water at |dy + 216| < 17, |dx| < 210, which has no physics body.
- **Authored events** (D51), from `MechanismPhysics`, saved and emitted by `Adventure.step` as `assembly` events with the responsible traveler:
  - `gate:latched` / `gate:closed` (the motor target 0 ↔ ±1.6 is saved);
  - `launcher:cocked` / `launcher:fired` (zone props launched at 420 units/s × impulse strength and instigated by the cocker);
  - `bridge:span-lost`;
  - `<kind>:<joint>:snapped|cut`.

  Chain, vine and vane are physical only.
- **Gameplay integration**: Whorl, Nova, slashes, dash, mechanics and enemy charges push members through `damageProps` (with instigation). Grab/hold/throw work on loose members: holds pull, throws yank. Planks and vanes are neither cover nor grabbable. Rifts carry a held unanchored part whole (`AdventurePhysics.carry` → `PhysicsWorld.transport`); an anchored part releases the hold. `place` on a jointed member moves its whole part. Waves never start inside a gate pen.
- **Policies and off/portal behavior**:
  - `mechanisms` off freezes every jointed part where it stands and ignores impulses on it. Parts already cut free stay loose. Re-enabling wakes a part whole with zero velocity, or blocks reactivation while any member overlaps something outside the part.
  - `jointBreakage` off prevents snaps and cuts and keeps existing breaks.
  - `jointStrength` (0.05–20) scales both thresholds.
  - Parts resolve policy at their root, and checkpoint validation enforces it (no half chains).
  - Presets: Quiet turns both off, Sanctuary turns breakage off, Wild sets strength 0.5.
  - Portals: lands archive assemblies, joint states and mechanism state with their props.
- **Versions**: physical world **7** (`assemblies`, `joints`), adventure envelope **5** (`mechanisms`; archives gain `assemblies`/`joints`/`mechanisms`), room protocol **8**. M06 checkpoints (envelope 4 / world 6) migrate: mechanisms are added to the active land once, and archived pre-M07 lands gain them on return. A real M06 checkpoint is a test fixture.
- **Agent**:
  - `actors` actions: `mechanisms` (guests too), `cut`, `motor`, `transport`.
  - `physics` actions: lab `assembly`, `cut` and `motor`.
  - The guest page allows the read-only `mechanisms`.
