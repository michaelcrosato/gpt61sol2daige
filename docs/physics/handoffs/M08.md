# M08 handoff

## Delivered behavior

Every adventure area now has a **reaction yard**: a brazier, two oil jars, a five-segment dry-brush fuse ending at a powder keg, two water casks, two conductor rods, a storm coil and a fan. A gusting **wind lane** also blows over the meadow vane. The yard sits 104+ units south of the entry–portal route, beside the clearing where fights happen. The existing volatile barrel near the clearing centre now explodes too.

- **Fire.** Burning strikes (Cinderwake's buff or a burn skill) ignite flammable scenery; a body pressed against a lit brazier catches. Burning props lose durability every half second and spread fire to touching neighbours. Dry brush crumbles to ash; wood chars and never burns again. Oil makes even metal and stone burn.
- **Water and oil.** A broken cask spills a puddle; a broken jar spills a slick, and a jar that catches fire bursts into a burning slick. Puddles, the creek and water stimuli soak bodies and put fires and lit fuses out. Fire on a wet body only boils water off (steam).
- **Electricity.** A struck storm coil (or a Stormglass activation, or a broken glass pylon) discharges. The discharge hops through metal, wet bodies and puddles, weaker each hop, shocking monsters (more if wet), cracking glass and ceramic and sparking any keg it touches.
- **Explosions.** A lit fuse (50 ticks) or a broken keg explodes: a short pressure field throws props, debris, loot and monsters outward, blast damage falls off over 100 units, then fire and sympathetic detonations follow as propagation.
- **Fields.**
  - A struck fan blows a strong lane for 4 s; wind lanes push by material windage and spin the vane.
  - Gravity Knots also gather props, debris and loot.
  - Riftstep arrivals repel.
  - Slipstream activations swirl a vortex.
- **Causality.** Every reaction belongs to a chain that records its owner, depth, rules and visited targets. Kills, broken props and rewards are credited to whoever started the chain.
- **Controls.** `materialReactions`, `chainReactions`, `environmentalForces` and `fieldStrength`, per land, area and region, with presets and panel controls (see the [policy section](../POLICIES.md#m08-delivered-controls)).

## Contracts and source

- **Registry** (`src/physics/reactions.ts`): `RULES` has 16 rows: ignite, steam, extinguish, soak, coat, flare, spread, burn, burnout, dry, conduct, detonate, spill, release, field and heat. Each row names its trigger, whether it is propagation, and every parameter. `REACTIVITY` gives each material's fuel and windage. Containers: casks hold water, jars hold oil. Releases: lanterns release fire, pylons release shock. `reactionExport()` serves agents and docs.
- **State** (`ReactionState`, saved in adventure envelope 6 as `reactions`):
  - statuses per prop or `enemy-<id>`: remaining `burning`, `wet`, `oiled`, `charged`, `fuse` and `heat` ticks, `charred`, chain, depth, owner and team;
  - surfaces, fields and delayed reactions (each with its source's `areaId`);
  - chains (owner, origin, rules, visited `rule|target` keys, depth, event count);
  - a 96-event history, pending events and damage, coil cooldowns and a sequence counter.
- **Causal model** (D55, D56):
  - A primary stimulus starts a chain; a rule applies to a target once per chain.
  - Direct effects (the struck body, a blast's push and damage, a spill's splash, a discharge at its source) happen whatever `chainReactions` says.
  - Propagation is depth + 1 and checked at its source both when queued and every tick while pending: spread, burning slicks lighting what walks in, blast fire and sympathetic detonations, and discharge hops.
  - Termination comes from finite fuel and fuses, strength decay (×0.85 per hop, 0.2 floor), dedup, and charred or spent bodies.
- **Paused timers** (D57): statuses and surfaces hold remaining-tick counters, so `materialReactions` off simply stops decrementing them. A pending effect aimed at a paused body waits with its original id. Land archives keep reactions with relative fuse and cooldown timers and drop monster statuses.
- **Fields** (D58): pre-solve `PhysicsWorld.fieldPush` velocity changes.
  - Props and loot: wind is scaled by windage; the other fields act uniformly.
  - Actors take decaying external motion: players ×0.5, bosses ×0.2.
  - The vane rotor takes wind as spin.
  - Field owners instigate what they move, so blown props hitting monsters are credited.
- **Integration**:
  - `AdventurePhysics.solve` applies fields before the step and runs `reactions.update` after mechanisms.
  - `damageProps` strikes coils and fans and carries an attack's `element` (burning strikes).
  - `breakProp` calls `reactions.broke` (spill, release, detonate, fire carried into pieces).
  - `Adventure.step` applies reaction damage through `hit` and `damageProps` with the chain id, and emits `reaction` events. Soak and coat stay in the history.
  - Mechanic hooks: Stormglass discharges, Gravity Knots attract, Riftstep repels, Slipstream swirls.
- **Versions** (D60): physical world **8** (policy samples gain the four controls; older samples are recomputed and proven), adventure envelope **6**, room protocol **9**. A real M07 checkpoint (`tests/fixtures/m07-raw.json.gz`, captured from `main` `fcba70a`) migrates: yards and wind lanes join the active land once, and the archived pre-M08 land gains them on return.
- **Agent**:
  - `actors` actions: `reactions` (guests too), `stimulate` (fire, water, oil, shock or blast on one body or an area) and `field` (add or remove).
  - `body` now returns the body's reaction status.
  - All are listed in `describe`.
- **Presentation** (`src/render/reactions.ts`, `props.ts`):
  - art for seven new families;
  - status overlays: flames, drips, oil sheen, crackle, fuse sparks and char;
  - puddles and slicks, wind streaks, vortex, attract and pressure rings;
  - event arcs, blasts, splashes, steam puffs and labels;
  - six synthesized voices (ignite, hiss, zap, boom, splash, gust).
- **Efficiency** (D53, "reasonably efficient where it is easy"):
  - `synchronizeTerrain` reads prop positions directly instead of cloning every prop's pose each tick;
  - the renderer and grab hints use clone-free `PhysicsWorld.view` poses.
