# M06 handoff

## Delivered behavior

Combat now has physical consequences that change choices without changing the controls or progression. Every ability shoves and spins loose props through one shared interaction spec:
- the slash combo builds to a hard-launching finisher;
- Whorl flings props outward and Bloom Nova flings them hardest;
- Thornlance knocks what it pierces;
- dash shoulders props out of the way.

A prop launched by an attack, a throw, or another launched prop belongs to its instigator for 2.5 s. When it meets a monster fast enough, the monster takes impact damage through the ordinary hit path, so XP, gold, drops and procs are granted once, exactly as for a sword hit. An unowned rolling object can still hurt a monster but invents no hero credit or rewards. Enemy charges plough props ahead of them, and those props hurt heroes.

Thornlance pierces soft scenery, breaking pots and glass for free and spending a pierce on wood. It stops on stone, metal, stumps and rooted terrain unless the hero has ricochets, in which case it bounces. Enemy shots treat props and rooted terrain as cover. Deep water is never cover.

Travelers grab a loose prop with V, the Grab hotbar button or a touch tap. They carry it in front of them along their aim, throw it with the attack input, or set it down with V. Gear and three existing skill nodes add physical force, shatter strength and Thornlance ricochets.

Loot launches from kills and broken scenery, bounces off terrain and props for half a second, then the magnet takes it. Drops that come to rest somewhere unreachable are moved to the nearest reachable ground. Four new region policies cover impact damage, impact strength, projectile-world collision and physical loot.

## Contracts and source

- **Interaction schema** (`src/game/interactions.ts`): `ATTACKS[kind]` = `{kind, team: party|enemy|world, query: arc|circle|sweep|projectile|contact, impulse (units/s), torque (rad/s), material (scenery damage multiplier), scenery: cover|pierce|phase, pierce, ricochet, summary}` for `slash0..2`, `whorl`, `nova`, `lance`, `dash`, `bloom`, `mechanic` (bramble, rift, glass, echo and delayed effects), `charge`, `shot` and `impact`. `attackRecipe(cause)` resolves a cause. `HARD_MATERIALS` (stone, metal) and `FRAGILE_MATERIALS` set Thornlance rules. Impact damage is `max(0, closing − 110) × mass × 0.09 × impactStrength`; ownership lasts 150 ticks and a prop/target pair repeats only after 30 ticks. `attackExport()` backs `actors/attacks`.
- **Wiring:** `Adventure.damageArea` passes the spec's impulse, torque and material to `AdventurePhysics.damageProps`, scaled by the hero's `force`/`shatter`. `PropAttack` gains `impulse`, `torque`, `material`, `team` and `once` (strike each prop once per charge). Dash and enemy charges call `damageProps` directly. `Adventure.projectileScenery` sweeps each projectile through `AdventurePhysics.sceneryHit` → `PhysicsWorld.castScenery` (Rapier shape casts). Fracture pieces inherit the launch velocity and the breaker's ownership.
- **Attribution** (`src/physics/combat.ts`, `CombatPhysics`): `instigate(prop, owner, team, cause)` on every push or throw, and on hold, chain impacts and fracture pieces. Before each solve, fast props (≥110 units/s) are sampled. After it, started contacts with monsters, heroes or props become `PendingImpact {id, tick, prop, target, owner, team, damage, closing, x, y, angle}` under team rules: party and unowned props hurt monsters, enemy props hurt heroes, and anything hurts props. `Adventure.step` applies them at the next boundary through `hit`, `damageHero` (×0.6) or `damageProps`. `kill(owner "")` on a non-boss counts the death but grants no credit or drops; a boss still clears the area.
- **Input changes:** actions `{type: "grab", id}` and `{type: "release", throw}` run through `Adventure.action` (host-validated and acknowledged for guests). The drag target is the traveler's existing aim input (`aimX/aimY`, else facing). The host checks reach (72), mass (≤ 8), dynamics, region freeze and other holders. Held props carry a saved `held` flag, so they pass through travelers but still meet monsters. Browser: V toggles, attack (LMB/J/attack button) throws while holding, the `#grab-button` serves touch, and prompts read `[V] Grab <variant>` / `[LMB / J] Throw · [V] Set down`. `G` keeps its game-mode role.
- **Physical affixes:** `StatId` adds `force`, `shatter` and `ricochet`; `HeroStats.force/shatter` are multipliers and `ricochet` is an integer. Skill nodes keep their tree positions: Edgecraft (blade-6) +15% shatter, Ironwood (root-7) +20% force and Static Charge (gale-4) +1 ricochet per rank. Magic+ items roll an extra physical affix by a separate hash, so existing rolls keep their affixes; ricochet appears on weapons only.
- **Physical loot:** loot bodies `loot-<dropId>` (actor kind `loot`, radius 3/5, restitution 0.5, damping 2.4) meet terrain and props only. They launch on their drop's tick, get `LOOT_SETTLE_TICKS = 30` before the magnet (gold/XP) removes the body, and are recovered by ring search when resting on deep water, rooted decor or fixed scenery. With physics off, a drop has no body and is recovered immediately.
- **Policies:** `impactDamage`, `projectileWorld`, `physicalLoot` (booleans, gated by world reactions) and `impactStrength` (0–10). Quiet turns all three off; Sanctuary turns impact damage off; Wild sets impact strength ×2.
- **Versions:** adventure envelope **4** (`combat {instigators, impacts, suppressed, holds, settled}`), physical world **6** (props accept loot contacts; older raw bytes restore through the semantic rebuild), room protocol **7**, physical wire 2. M05 (envelope 3 / world 5) and older checkpoints migrate; a real M05 checkpoint is a test fixture.
- **Agent:** `actors` actions `attacks` (also on guests) and `inspect.combat`; adventure actions grab/release; guests' replica `inspect` includes `combat`.
