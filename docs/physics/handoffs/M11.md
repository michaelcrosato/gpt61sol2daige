# M11 handoff

## Delivered behavior

Areas beyond the eighth are composed from a physical encounter grammar instead of repeating the authored areas' layout with new colours.

- **Encounter grammar.** Each generated area takes a plan from its recipe:
  - two clusters (three from area 21), each one of 14 compatibility rules binding modules into a working chain;
  - one standalone mechanism module;
  - combat pockets where its waves gather beside the clusters;
  - the essential arrival → warden → exit route plus an advantage route through each cluster;
  - a regional profile per cluster that strengthens its reaction;
  - a composed warden.

  | Combination | What happens | Measured (receipt) |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Stormcatch pool | a struck coil's discharge runs down conductor rods into a pool | wading crawler 600 → 577 HP |
  | Shattered spark | a broken pylon at a pool's edge discharges through the water | wading crawler 600 → 569 HP |
  | Burning palisade | a jar thrown into the brazier's coals bursts, the slick lights a brush fuse, the weakened stockade burns open | burnt through 155 ticks after the jar met the coals |
  | Oil flare | a jar shoved into the coals sets the oil cellar ablaze | 2 jars burning |
  | Powder trail | a fuse ends at a powder store | 2/2 barrels blown |
  | Briar blaze | a fuse feeds a thorn-hedge line | hedge line burnt through |
  | Quench trough | casks over a fuse: break one and the fire goes out | last brush unburnt |
  | Rubble maelstrom | a vortex with a pull swirls rubble and monsters | 4/4 pieces carried |
  | Powder vortex | powder barrels orbit the eye; one spark blows them all | 2/2 together |
  | Debris gale | a wind lane rolls rubble into the pocket | 4/4 pieces carried |
  | Powder battery | a launcher fires its stone into powder | 2/2 blown |
  | Battering launcher | a launcher batters a stockade open | front board 45 → 0 |
  | Wrecking ball | a chained ball swings through glass, which discharges | pylon shattered, `release` + `conduct` |
  | Rift freight train | a cargo train (wagon, crate, barrel on ropes) rides a rift arch, joints intact | 3/3 through, 2 ropes intact |

- **Placement with fallbacks.** Clusters are placed whole so their chain links hold, and every link is checked with the pieces actually kept. A cluster that cannot stand moves to a spare slot, then uses alternate modules, then the next combination; with no room anywhere, its slot stays open. Every fallback is written to the manifest. Placement keeps routes walkable with all pieces treated as solid, keeps clear of hazards (brazier heat, lane run-outs, pools over dry pieces, fire pieces on water) and never starts bodies overlapped.
- **Modular wardens.**
  - The warden's title names its armor ("Violet Harbinger, Barkbound").
  - Its signature moves come from the area's mechanics (two; three from area 21) and rotate each slam turn. Any of their weaknesses exposes it.
  - It wears 2–4 armor pieces on tethers: bark plates, glass shards, boulders or iron censers. Each takes 12% of damage while it hangs within 64 units.
  - Pieces break by material (fire, shock, blasts, launched props), tethers snap under a yank, and all of them fall loose when the warden dies.
  - Two arena kits ring its ground: one that strips its armor, one that serves a weakness (pillars, braziers, conductor pool, flood pool, hanging weights, siege launcher, powder kegs).
- **Guidance.** Cluster rings carry each combination's name in its profile's colour. The mechanic guide lists the encounter and how to start each combination, plus the warden's moves, weaknesses, armor and its counter, and its arena. The HUD shows `ARMORED ×N`.
- **Agent tools.** `encounters`:
  - `catalog`;
  - `preview`: builds any area without a game;
  - `validate`: visible errors for unknown references, impossible placements and manifests that do not reproduce;
  - `export`: the land as built, plus its mutations.
