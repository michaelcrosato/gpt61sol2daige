# M05 handoff

## Delivered behavior

Every clearing now holds readable, breakable scenery: four crates and a wheel (M03), two barrels (one volatile), two pots, a log, two loose stones, a wagon, a three-segment fence, a glass pylon, a lantern and three trees. That is 84 props per land, each drawn with its own silhouette and with land-specific construction for the five palettes. Ordinary attacks (slash, Whorl, Nova, Bloom, Bramble, Rift and delayed effects) push loose props and damage them through their material. A crate splinters into four planks and pays a one-time gold drop. A glass pylon shatters into five shards in one hit. Stone resists a weak hit (`RESIST`) and chips under a strong one. A tree falls away from the attacker as a fixed stump plus a dynamic log that can be pushed and broken again. A wagon drops planks, its cloth canopy and two working wheels.

Damage stages show as cracks. Canopies and wagon covers sway and shudder. Particles (splinters, dust, shards, sparks, fibres, leaves) and one synthesized voice per material give feedback on host and guests. Destruction, material toughness and debris lifetime are regional policies. Destroyed state survives save/load, replay, recall, portable rebuilds, connected guests and late joiners.

## Contracts and source

- **Materials** (`src/physics/materials.ts`): `MATERIALS[id]` = density, friction, restitution, damping, `resistance` (damage removed per hit), `vulnerability` (multiplier), `burst` (fracture speed), `knockback`, `feedback`, three colors, plus `flammability`/`conductivity` (recorded for M08, unused). `materialDamage(material, damage, scale) = max(0, damage - resistance) * vulnerability / max(0.05, scale)`.
- **Blueprints** (`src/physics/blueprints.ts`): `PROP_FAMILIES` (13), `FAMILIES[family]` = five variant names, material/trim, motion, shape, mass scale, `toughness` (damage from intact to broken; 0 = cannot break), base `reward`, authored disjoint `pieces`. `propRecipe` derives mass/friction/restitution/damping from the material. `CLEARING_LAYOUT` + `clearingProps(area, palette, blocked)` place 16 props per area, turning around the center to avoid solid terrain (`solidTerrain` in `terrain.ts`). `blueprintExport()` is the reproducible registry (`actors/recipes`).
- **Persistent mutation keys:** body recipe `material`, `blueprint {family, palette, piece?, parent?, expiresAt?}` and `consequences.durability` (percent; absent for stumps/debris). Stages: >66.7 intact, >33.3 cracked, >0 failing, 0 destroyed. `AdventurePhysics.destroyed` holds `DestroyedRecord {id, family, palette, material, x, y, angle, tick, owner, cause, reward, pieces}` per land. Archives carry `destroyed`. `isDestroyed(id)` IDs are retired: validation rejects bodies and archived props under them, and agent spawns refuse them.
- **Fracture ownership:** gameplay (`Adventure.damageArea` → `AdventurePhysics.damageProps`) decides damage after the unchanged enemy hit, in stable ID order, at a command boundary. `breakProp` removes the parent, spawns `<parent>-<kind>` pieces with inherited velocity plus material burst (tree: along the hit angle), and records the parent. A frozen region spawns frozen pieces. Contacts never damage props (M06), and Thornlance projectiles ignore scenery (M06).
- **Reward rule:** a record's `reward = round(base * (1 + min(area, 500) * 0.13))` (crate 3, barrel 4, pot 5, wagon 9, lantern 2; others 0). `Adventure.propEffects` drops it once as party gold at the break site and emits `break` (`family:material`) or up to six `impact` (`material:resisted|protected|stage<n>`) events.
- **Policies** (`src/physics/policies.ts`): `destruction` (effective = worldReactions && destruction), `materialDurability` 0.05–20, `debrisLifetime` 0–3,600 s (sampled at break time into `expiresAt`; 0 = scene lifetime; debris family only; removed in `begin()` in sorted order). Presets: Quiet/Sanctuary destruction off; Wild 0.6× toughness. Agent lab adds the three controls.
- **Versions:** outer save 2, adventure envelope **3** (`destroyed`), physical world **5**, policy document 1, room protocol **6**, physical wire 2. `upgradeAdventurePhysics` + `upgradePolicySamples` migrate v1/v2 envelopes/world v3–4. They verify old policy fields before re-resolving, annotate legacy crates/wheels as wood (pose key order preserved for raw continuation), and add absent clearing props once to active and archived lands.
- **Presentation:** `src/render/props.ts` (`PropRenderer`, `drawMaterialEvent`), `src/render/renderer.ts`, `src/render/combat.ts`, `src/audio/synth.ts` (`MATERIAL_SOUNDS`, `crumble`), `src/main.ts` routing, generated WAVs in `public/generated/`.
- **Agent:** `actors` actions `props`, `recipes` (also on guests) and host-only `damage {id, damage, angle?}` → `Adventure.strikeProp`.
