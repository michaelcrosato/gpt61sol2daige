## Contracts and source

- Save **2**, adventure physics envelope **2**, physical world **4**, policy document **1**, room protocol **5**, physical wire **2**. `src/physics/{types,runtime,adventure}.ts` retains complete solved pose/velocity/sleep/freeze/CCD, recipes, applied policy samples/provenance and motor intent/controlled/external state. Queued policy edits, gameplay delayed effects, archives and pending terrain boundaries survive.
- Normal saves use checksummed pinned Rapier **0.21.0** bytes for exact same-build continuation. `continuation: rebuild` omits raw bytes; a changed backend version also selects complete semantic rebuilding. Corrupt compatible bytes still reject. Rebuild rotation drift is below **0.00001 radians** in the demonstrated scene; contact caches are reconstructed and later trajectory comparisons use physical outcomes/tolerances. Gameplay facts stay exact. Old replays belong to their recording engine revision. Old version-1 co-op solver flags also migrate to fresh authoritative physics; paused fractional distant teleports publish the solved owner pose before saving.
- `validateSave` / runtime validation checks schemas, identities, memberships, motors, seed/land/game pose agreement and semantic terrain before allocating a solver or publishing a replica. Compatible binary restore additionally checks mappings/properties/shapes/groups. `Adventure.configureTerrain` shares world configuration with validation; decoration policy bindings use the original tile-center sample. Paused terrain edits retain their explicit pending boundary.
- `src/net/physical.ts` carries a complete portable checkpoint and reliable scene/spawn/remove/policy events outside the observational packet's unchanged **512,000-byte** header budget. Manifest: version, sequence, scene revision, compressed/expanded UTF-8 length/count/checksum. Chunks: **48,000 bytes** maximum, total **256,000,000 bytes**. Both PeerJS ArrayBuffer/Uint8Array forms are supported. Every selected ambient creature and physical body is sent; no interest or FPS eligibility cutback exists.
- One staged receiver accepts equal duplicates/reversed chunk order after the reliable manifest, rejects conflicts/corruption/stale framing, and never publishes an incomplete world. New manifests supersede staging. Native gzip preserves the complete scene, with bounded inflation. Over-bound scenes produce an explicit export/checkpoint-loading error.
- Host frame scheduling (`frameChoice` in `src/net/physical.ts`, used by `src/net/coop.ts`): one population-sized build serves every guest, at most one is in flight, and builds are spaced by twice their measured cost (at least **100 ms**). A guest receives the newest frame above its floor: its last acknowledgement, or at admission the sequence before the next build, so it never receives a frame predating its body. An accepted action forces a newer build. The host binds a new traveler's body with `begin()` once at admission; repeated hellos only repeat the welcome, and guests stop sending hellos once welcomed. Large scenes transmit below the nominal 10 Hz cadence without changing simulation participation or eligibility.
- Transfer pacing and liveness: chunks refill up to **256 KiB** of native buffered data on every host update and on `bufferedamountlow` (**64 KiB**). A 1 MiB refill was measured to starve a loaded host's admission of new connections. A guest sends `physical-staged` once every chunk is held, before decoding; the host never resends a staged transfer and resends an unstaged one, identical, only after **10 s** without sending. The host sends `alive` to every admitted guest about once per second (**1 Hz**); a connected guest continues solo only after **10 s** without any host frame, chunk or heartbeat. Join attempts (6/10/22 s) measure baseline progress or, once welcomed, the in-order host heartbeat; the earlier blind five-second manifest resend had served that role by accident. Older room-5 peers ignore the new messages; room protocol **5** / physical wire **2** framing is unchanged.
- `Simulation.applyReplica` retains the complete received scene without a new Rapier world per frame and refuses authoritative stepping. Rendering uses received ambient ownership and shortest-path prop angular interpolation. Seed/run/transition/land changes snap actors/projectiles/props. Full replicated checkpoints include all party inventories; the separate observational packet helper still supports bounded interest and reduced other-player inventories. Bounded quest/pulse observation events replicate but remain outside replay hashes.
- `interact-physics` (host/solo agent) and `window.fern.network.interact({id,x,y,atX?,atY?})` (guest RPC) accept an existing movable prop within **96 units**, impulse components **±120**, and paired application coordinates within **32 units** of the prop. Connection identity is authoritative. Unknown/extra fields, actors and far/excessive requests reject. Frozen-region impulses are discarded. Guests have no policy-write RPC; host UI/agent edits use the existing revision queue. `actors` inspect/body/policy also works on replicas.
- `Simulation.continueSolo` retains the selected hero and complete received population/scene, removes departed bodies/contact references before rekeying to `local`, and rekeys owned delayed/projectile work. Replacements free superseded worlds/queues. Browser unload closes transport without constructing an unused recovery scene; repeated pagehide is harmless.
- `tools/agent.ts` supports discoverable CLI-only `restore-file` with a checkpoint path outside the unchanged **8 MB** JSONL command bound. Browser IndexedDB and JSON exports keep complete saves.
