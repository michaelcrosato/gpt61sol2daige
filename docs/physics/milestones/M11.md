# M11 Procedural physical encounters and bosses

Dependency: M10 verified and merged. Extend the world beyond authored areas using the same material, mechanism, rig and reaction vocabulary.

## Steps

1. Add physical encounter grammar to area recipes: prop groups, materials, fields, assemblies, regional profiles, entrances, combat pockets and optional advantage routes. Separate seed-generated initial content from persistent mutations.
2. Define compatibility rules that produce intentional combinations, such as conductor layouts near water, ignition chains near dry structures, gravity fields with loose mass and portals with transportable assemblies.
3. Compose boss behavior, body/armor assemblies, breakable attachments and arena interactions from reusable modules. Ensure palette swaps are not the sole source of encounter variation.
4. Generate valid collision placement and essential routes. If a generated arrangement is unsuitable, choose another valid recipe or retain an alternate path; never require a precise simulated outcome to finish.
5. Add catalog, preview, validate and export operations to the existing agent tools. A scene manifest should name selected modules, policies and relevant causal rules so another agent can reproduce it cheaply.

## Acceptance

- Inspect a fixed seed corpus across all five themes and a range of depths, including a large area index. Show at least twelve distinct meaningful combinations and several boss assemblies.
- At least three generated areas beyond area eight are completed through normal inputs; one demonstrates a multi-system interaction and one finishes with reactions disabled.
- Initial recipes reproduce from seed, while saved broken/moved state remains authoritative on restoration. Invalid references or impossible placements fail validation visibly.
- Exercise a generated encounter with an actual guest and after save/load. No new hidden physics body cap or FPS-driven substitution is introduced.

## Handoff

Record grammar, compatibility/placement rules, seed corpus, generated boss modules and tool examples. Push/merge; write `handoffs/M11.md` and reset before M12.
