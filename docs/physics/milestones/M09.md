# M09 Physical rigs and expressive reactions

Dependency: M08 verified and merged. Deliver coherent physical animation across the existing six monster rigs while keeping the main character recognizable and responsive.

## Steps

1. Extend rig recipes with physical sockets, limb/armor parts, mass distribution and limits. Keep locomotion/attack intent authored; use secondary motion and constraint targets to add weight and impact response.
2. Implement hit recoil, stagger, topple and death transitions for crawler, stalker, brute, wraith, totem and warden. Grounded, floating and rooted rigs must retain their distinct character.
3. Transfer pose and momentum into physical death assemblies without running the living actor solver twice. Detachable bark/armor pieces can become material-aware props; dead bodies cannot keep attacking or duplicating loot.
4. Give the wayfarer and town NPCs controlled recoil, clothing/lantern motion and readable contact feedback. Preserve the wayfarer's silhouette and input control. Essential services recover from harmless motion rather than becoming unavailable.
5. Integrate physical poses into y-sorting, shadows, lighting, material sounds and screen feedback. Expose ragdoll, foliage and reaction-strength controls plus local camera-shake/flash preferences.

## Acceptance

- Each of the six rigs has an inspected moving capture showing a distinct hit and death response, not merely a differently colored rigid rectangle.
- A dead body reacts to Whorl or a field, then correctly freezes/settles when ragdolls are disabled. Saving/loading cannot revive the enemy or repeat its reward.
- Physical limbs do not separate from their visible art unexpectedly; interpolation, joint limits and ground contact remain legible at close and ordinary zoom.
- The player still responds immediately, and NPC services remain usable. Desktop and mobile show the same gameplay outcome, and a guest replica receives the same poses and results.
