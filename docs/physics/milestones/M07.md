# M07 Jointed mechanisms and assemblies

Dependency: M06 verified and merged. Deliver objects made of connected physical parts, with interactions that persist when anchors break or features change.

## Steps

1. Add assembly recipes with sockets/anchors, member bodies, joint definitions, motor targets and broken-joint state. Support hinges, fixed attachments, tethers and springs through verified Rapier APIs or an explicitly documented game rule where needed.
2. Build a swinging gate, a chained obstacle, a flexible vine/rope, a sprung launcher, a rotating wind vane and a bridge or causeway mechanism suitable for the top-down plane.
3. Implement cutting/freeing anchors and breakable joints using declared stress/damage thresholds. If exact joint force readback is unavailable, use observable displacement/contact stress with a documented approximation; scientific precision is not required.
4. Integrate Whorl, pulling, throwing and enemy charges with assemblies. Distinguish mechanisms that move only physically from those that emit an authored gameplay event.
5. Apply policies to whole assemblies at their root. Freeze/re-enable without an impulse explosion, teleport eligible unanchored assemblies as a unit, and retain broken connections through save/network transfer.

## Acceptance

- A gate swings around its hinge, a tether limits motion, and a broken anchor releases the connected object with visible inherited motion.
- Switching joints or joint breakage off has the documented distinct effect. A region boundary cannot leave half a chain in a contradictory state.
- Required travel stays reachable if a mechanism jams or physics is disabled; optional shortcuts remain worthwhile.
- Assembly membership, broken links and motor state survive save/load and an actual guest joining mid-interaction.

## Handoff

Record supported joint types, any stress approximation, assembly recipes and off/portal behavior. Push/merge; write `handoffs/M07.md` and reset before M08.
