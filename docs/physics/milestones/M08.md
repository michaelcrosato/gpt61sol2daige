# M08 Material reactions and environmental fields

Dependency: M07 verified and merged. Deliver composable reactions across materials, objects, enemies and regional forces.

## Steps

1. Implement a data-driven reaction registry with heat/fire, water/wetness, oil, electricity and vegetation states. Add extinguishing, ignition, conductive discharge, break-triggered release and timed material transitions.
2. Introduce wind lanes/fans, radial pressure, attraction, repulsion and vortex fields using the shared force API. Fields affect eligible props, fragments, assemblies, loot and actors according to policy and material.
3. Add reusable water/oil containers, conductive metal pieces, fuse-like vegetation and volatile barrels. Use understandable game surfaces and body interactions rather than requiring a full fluid solver.
4. Compose primary and secondary events with ownership, remaining timers, strength decay and causal deduplication. A chain must terminate without a hidden FPS-based truncation mechanism.
5. Provide material and chain-reaction controls, field tuning, readable status/area feedback and distinct audio. Save and replicate active statuses, field definitions and delayed events.

## Acceptance

- Demonstrate ignition, extinguishing, wet/metal conduction and an explosion moving other objects. Demonstrate a wind or gravity field bending the result of a fight.
- Show one reproducible chain involving at least three different rule types, such as impulse → spill → discharge → breakage. Inspect its event causes and reward attribution.
- Turning propagation off stops secondary reactions; turning material reactions off suspends optional material evolution without disabling existing combat abilities. Re-enable produces no accumulated event burst.
- The same reaction remains coherent after a mid-chain save and when observed by real co-op guests.

## Handoff

Record rule registry, causal model, paused-timer behavior, field recipes and demonstrated chains. Push/merge; write `handoffs/M08.md` and reset before M09.
