# M06 Combat forces projectiles and physical loot

Dependency: M05 verified and merged. Deliver physical interactions that change combat choices while preserving the existing controls and progression.

## Steps

1. Define a shared attack interaction specification: query shape, authored damage, impulse, torque, material damage, pierce/ricochet behavior and owner. Wire slash combos, Whorl, Thornlance, Bloom Nova, dash and enemy charges into it.
2. Use sweeps for eligible fast projectiles and attacks. Scenery can block, break, deflect or be pierced according to material/ability rules. Preserve intentional phasing abilities and explicit off-state behavior.
3. Add physical impact damage from launched props with meaningful relative motion/contact strength, recent instigator ownership, per-target hit suppression and team rules. Attribute kills, XP and loot exactly once through the existing reward path.
4. Add grab/drag and release/throw for suitable loose props through one discoverable interaction mode, with keyboard/mouse and touch equivalents. Preserve E pickup priority and do not overload ordinary movement with a hidden gesture. Send continuous drag targets through the validated input stream; acknowledge discrete grab/release actions and check ownership/distance on the host.
5. Let gear/skills expose physical modifiers through existing affix/skill systems: impulse, shatter strength or ricochet count with concrete effects. Do not replace the 48-node tree with a new unrelated progression system.
6. Give loot a physical launch/settle phase and reliable magnet/pickup behavior. Recover valuable drops from inaccessible terrain. Add numeric force/impact controls and update network/save contracts for ownership and pending hits.

## Acceptance

- A thrown or Whorl-launched crate damages a monster and grants the appropriate party rewards once. An unowned rolling object does not invent player kill credit.
- Thornlance and ordinary enemy shots honor their authored cover/pierce rules; toggling projectile-world collision affects scenery without disabling base combat hits.
- Grab/throw is discoverable on desktop and touch. Physical loot settles, collects and survives saving; disabling its physics leaves it collectible.
- Combat still feels immediate under real held mouse and keyboard inputs, with repeatable evidence of each active ability's physical consequence.

Record interaction schema, attribution, input changes, physical affixes and off-state cases. Push/merge; write `handoffs/M06.md` and reset before M07.
